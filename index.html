Privacy Policy

App: Curse of Scully: Pirate Island
Developer: Party Beast Games
Privacy contact: lauri.lille@gmail.com
Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Party Beast Games and our service providers handle information when you play Curse of Scully, use its online features, make purchases or contact us. Older builds may not include every feature described below.

Usage analytics is optional and off by default. In builds that include the Privacy screen, you can allow or decline it and change your choice later. Declining usage analytics does not prevent you from playing. This choice is separate from account services, cloud saves, purchases, bug reports, crash reporting and notifications.
Optional analytics · Account and data deletion · Retention · Contact

1. Accounts and cloud saves

Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, including guest accounts, email-based accounts and Google sign-in. Depending on the sign-in method, we process an account identifier, email address and display name. Email-account credentials are sent to Firebase Authentication to authenticate you. Google sign-in is handled by Google; the game does not receive your Google password.

Game progress is saved locally and, when cloud services are used, on our Firebase servers. Cloud records include your progress, resources, ships, purchases or entitlements recorded in the save, gameplay timestamps and account identifiers. We use this information to save and restore progress, synchronize devices, operate online features and protect game integrity. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete an account or its server-side save.

2. Online gameplay and communications

Online features process your chosen player name, account identifier, in-game island position, alliance membership, rankings, actions and messages needed to run those features. Information displayed in shared game areas, rankings, alliances or chat can be visible to other players. An in-game island position is a game coordinate, not your physical location.

Our servers and service providers process technical request information, such as network addresses, timestamps and error information, to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. We do not request GPS location for gameplay.

3. Purchases

Google Play Billing processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase tokens, product identifiers and the game account identifier to verify purchases, check entitlements and support restoration. Purchase information may also be recorded in your game save. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.

4. Bug reports and support

When you submit an in-game report, we receive your message and attached diagnostics. Diagnostics include the game version, device and platform information, language, account identifier, account email when available, player name, selected progression information, game state and a recent activity-log excerpt. An optional screenshot can be attached; the report screen lets you preview it and remove that attachment before sending.

Reports are stored on Firebase and reviewed by the authorized development team to investigate issues and respond to feedback. Messages sent to our support email are processed to handle your request. Avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in messages or screenshots.

5. Crash reporting and notifications

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic information to investigate failures. This includes stack traces, device and operating-system information, app versions, installation identifiers and diagnostic breadcrumbs. Our integration can attach a game account identifier and player name so we can investigate a reported issue. Crash reporting is separate from optional usage analytics.

Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an app-instance registration token and its association with your account to deliver game notifications, such as raid alerts and game announcements. Android may ask for notification permission. You can control displayed notifications in Android's app settings. Turning off notifications does not delete your account.

6. Optional usage analytics

In builds containing usage analytics, collection is off until you select Allow usage analytics. Your choice is saved on that device. You can change it under Privacy on the main menu. Choosing to keep it off does not restrict gameplay or purchases.

If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase receives broad game-area names, such as Main Menu, Base, World Map, Grand Horizon, Exploration, Battle and Ocean Tycoon. Google also processes a random installation identifier, app and device information, automatically collected app events, sessions and foreground engagement, and derives approximate geographic information from network connections. We use aggregate reports to understand activity, time spent in the app, feature usage and returning installations.

The screen events we add do not contain your account email, cloud-save contents, messages or screenshots. We do not set an Analytics user ID linked to your game account. Installation identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity; reinstalling or resetting Analytics can create a new identifier.

Our usage integration disables Analytics advertising-ID collection and denies advertising-storage, advertising-data and advertising-personalization consent. Allowing usage analytics does not opt you into advertising personalization.

Turning usage analytics off stops collection and resets local Analytics data and the installation identifier. It does not retroactively erase information already sent to Google or existing aggregate reports. For a privacy or deletion request, contact us below. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

7. Who receives information

Authorized members of Party Beast Games use account, gameplay, support and aggregate reporting information to operate and improve the game. Our private development dashboard is restricted to approved team access.

  • Google / Firebase: authentication, database and cloud services, crash reporting, notifications and optional usage analytics. Google Play also supplies store reports, such as installs, uninstalls and reviews. These store reports are separate from the game's usage-analytics choice. See Firebase privacy and security and Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification and entitlements. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Other players: information you make visible through online game features, as described above.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where needed to comply with law, address fraud or security issues, or protect users and the service. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

8. Retention and security

Account and cloud-save records are kept to maintain your account and game progress until you request deletion or the records are no longer needed. Support reports, online-game records and operational logs are retained for the time needed to investigate issues, operate features and protect the service. There is no single fixed retention period for all these records.

Google Analytics event data follows the linked property's retention settings. Aggregated reports can remain after the underlying event data expires. Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play and RevenueCat apply their own retention practices. Records necessary for legal obligations, transactions, fraud prevention or security may need to be retained after an account-deletion request; we will explain any applicable exception when handling the request.

Network communication with our cloud services uses HTTPS. Access to private records and development tools is restricted. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

9. Request account or data deletion

You can request deletion of your Curse of Scully account and associated cloud-save and game-account data by emailing lauri.lille@gmail.com with the subject Curse of Scully account deletion. This request is available without reinstalling the game. The Privacy screen in updated builds links to this section.

Send the request from the email associated with your account where possible. For a guest account, include your player name and any account identifier available in the game. We may need to verify ownership to avoid deleting someone else's progress. Do not send your password or payment-card information. We will explain what is deleted and any information that must be retained for the reasons above. Deleting an account is permanent and does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription; manage subscriptions through Google Play.

You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, or to exercise other privacy rights available under applicable law. You can withdraw usage-analytics consent in the game's Privacy screen. You may raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority.

10. Children's privacy

Curse of Scully is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted here with a new date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request a new choice before introducing a relevant change.
