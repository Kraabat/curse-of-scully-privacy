Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Curse of Scully is a mobile game developed by Party Beast Games. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected when you use our app and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to operate the app:

  • Purchase history: When you make an in-app purchase, our payment provider (RevenueCat) records the transaction to validate and restore your purchases. This data is processed by RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment details.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash logs

3. Game Save Data

Your game progress is saved locally on your device. It may also be backed up via Google Play Games cloud save if enabled on your device. We do not have access to your save data on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • RevenueCat – In-app purchase management. RevenueCat's privacy policy can be found at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing – Payment processing by Google. Google's privacy policy applies to all transactions made through Google Play.

5. Children's Privacy

Curse of Scully is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

All purchase data transmitted through our app is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
